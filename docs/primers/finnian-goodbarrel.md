# Finnian Goodbarrel
{: .no_toc }
*Halfling rogue, with a criminal past*

You've lived on the wrong side of Waterdeep's law, and it shows in how you see the city: who's watching, what's worth taking, and which doors stay unlocked.

- TOC
{:toc}

## You know the underworld
*Because: Criminal background · Thieves' Cant · Expertise in Stealth and Sleight of Hand*

You know the [Underworld](../lore/underworld.html) page at **Expert** depth. The highlights:

- **There's no thieves' guild.** Crime is split between rival outfits, and nobody trusts anyone. Freelancers like you get by on knowing whose turf you're standing on.
- **The Xanathar Guild** owns much of the Dock Ward and the sewers. Its thugs wear the eye sign. The boss is supposedly a beholder, and paranoid. Working Xanathar turf without permission is a quick way to get hurt.
- **The Zhentarim** are dressing up as respectable guards and traders. Street talk says they and the Xanathar Guild are quietly bleeding each other.
- **Fences** pay about a third of value, and less for anything recognizable. Most run a pawnshop or secondhand stall as a front.
- **Cant sigils:** chalk marks on doorframes and gutters flag safe houses, Watch-heavy streets, easy marks and doors with dogs. A circle with a line through it means "Xanathar territory, keep out."
- **Skullport** lies far below the city. It's lawless and will trade in anything. Easy to get to, hard to leave with your money.
- **Neverember's gold:** every crook claims a cousin who knows where it's hidden. None of them do.
- **Smugglers** use small boats at night, sea caves and old cellars. The Watch barely patrols the water.
- **The Death Curse has been good for business.** The Watch is stretched, the Lords are distracted, and there are fewer adventurers around to clean up the streets. Fake "cures" and stolen temple relics are moving fast. Everyone's desperate, and desperate people pay.

## You know the halfling side of the city
*Because: Halfling ancestry · speaks Gnomish*

From [Peoples of Waterdeep](../lore/races-cultures.html):

- A halfling can usually find a meal and a warm floor at another halfling's home, if you're polite and don't steal from your host.
- The **Shard Shunners** are a gang of halfling thieves working the sewers and cellars of the Dock Ward. You know of them, and they may well know of you.
- Gnomes in the Trades Ward (tinkers, jewelers, lens-makers) warm to a halfling who speaks their tongue. Useful when you need a lock understood, or a trinket appraised quietly.

## You know some history
*Because: History proficiency*

You know the [History](../lore/history.html) and [Nobility](../lore/nobility.html) pages at **Proficient** depth:

- The **Lords** have stayed secret since the wizard **Ahghairon** set up the system, so nobody can bribe or threaten them. The Open Lord is the exception.
- **Dagult Neverember** was removed because the other Lords got tired of him spending the treasury and living in Neverwinter. **Laeral Silverhand** is one of the legendary Seven Sisters, centuries old, and has ruled here before. Since the Death Curse she has all but vanished from public life, and the city is feeling her absence.
- With the treasury strained by the curse, **Neverember's stolen gold** isn't just a tavern story anymore. People with real power would very much like to find it.
- The city's old **thieves' guild** was destroyed by the Lords long ago. That's why the underworld is a free-for-all today.
- Noble houses are rich, proud, and well guarded, and their **servants know everything**. You know the major houses by name and reputation. Useful when choosing whose purse not to lift.

## You notice things
*Because: Investigation and Perception proficiency · Alert*

You know the [Places](../lore/places.html) page at **Expert** depth, from years of watching streets:

- Watch patrols are thinnest where wards meet, and their timing is predictable within a ward.
- Dock Ward and Trades Ward rooftops are close enough to cross on foot if you're nimble.
- Many cellars connect to the sewers, and some connect to each other in ways no plan shows.
- The Market is best for pickpockets at midday, when the crowd is thickest.
- **Trollskull Alley** in the North Ward has an old empty tavern that locals say is haunted.

## Languages

- **Common Sign Language:** handy for silent work.
- **Halfling** and **Gnomish:** see above.
