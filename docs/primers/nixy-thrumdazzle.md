# Nixy Thrumdazzle
{: .no_toc }
*Gnome bard and scholar*

You've read the histories, studied the arts arcane, and played the taverns. Waterdeep is a city of stories, and you know more of them than most.

## You know magic and its institutions
*Because: Sage background · Arcana proficiency · Magic Initiate (Wizard)*

You know the [Arcana](../lore/arcana.html) page at **Expert** depth:

- **The Watchful Order of Magists and Protectors** licenses, polices and supports spellcasters. It also staffs the city's magical defenses and answers magical emergencies. Members share libraries and spell supplies. As a dabbler in wizardry, you'd be welcome to join if you can pay the dues.
- **Blackstaff Tower** is home to the **Blackstaff**, the city's archmage, currently **Vajra Safahr**. The Blackstaff is traditionally the Lords' magical right hand. Rumor says she directs a small team of elite adventurers for the city.
- **Halaster Blackcloak**, the Mad Mage, still haunts **Undermountain** by most accounts. The dungeon reshapes itself, and its magical gates lead to strange places.
- **Ahghairon's Tower** has stood sealed since his death. Its wards supposedly let birds through and nothing else.
- **The walking statues** are giant stone guardians that defended the city in past crises. Scholars still argue about whether their enchantments are only sleeping.
- Magic items surface regularly in the markets, often from Undermountain. Selling dangerous ones openly draws the Watchful Order's attention.
- Old wizards' towers dot the city, sealed and warded. The wards are usually the least dangerous thing inside.

## You know the Death Curse as a scholar
*Because: Arcana proficiency*

You know the [Death Curse](../lore/death-curse.html) page at **Proficient** depth:

- It touches only those who were **raised from the dead**. Ordinary death works as it always did; it's just final now.
- The **wasting** is a slow countdown. The stronger the victim, the longer it takes, but nobody recovers. Greater restoration, remove curse and wish have all reportedly failed.
- Every scholar in the city has a theory, and you've heard most of them. None has been proven.

## You know the city's history
*Because: Sage background · History proficiency*

You know the [History](../lore/history.html) and [Nobility](../lore/nobility.html) pages at **Proficient** depth:

- **Ahghairon** founded the system of secret Lords, so nobody could bribe or threaten them. The Open Lord is the one public exception.
- **Laeral Silverhand** is one of the Seven Sisters, chosen of Mystra and centuries old. She once ruled here alongside **Khelben "Blackstaff" Arunsun**. For the past couple of months she has withdrawn from public life.
- **Dagult Neverember** was removed for spending the treasury and living in Neverwinter.
- **Halaster** built Undermountain long before the city grew over it.
- Noble houses are trading families with long memories. Notable ones: **Cassalanter** (banking, famously pious), **Wands** (wizards), **Amcathra** (horses), **Thann** (vineyards), **Rosznar** (wine, rebuilding a ruined name).

## You know the stages and the audiences
*Because: Performance · Investigation*

You know the [Places](../lore/places.html) page at **Proficient** depth:

- The **Yawning Portal** draws adventurers and their stories. It's quieter since the Death Curse, and the songs there have turned sadder.
- Performers make a living in the taverns and festhalls. The best stages pay coin; the rest pay in meals, tips and the occasional noble patron.
- Sea Ward **card salons** and noble winter balls hire musicians. That's good money, and a good place to overhear things.
- The **Market** is busiest at midday, which is good for busking and bad for your purse.
- **Trollskull Alley** in the North Ward has an old empty tavern locals say is haunted. Every performer in the city has heard a ghost story about it.

## You know your own people
*Because: Gnome ancestry*

From [Peoples of Waterdeep](../lore/races-cultures.html):

- Gnomes cluster around the city's workshops, alchemists and wizards, especially in the **Trades Ward**: tinkers, jewelers, lens-makers.
- Gnomes value clever work and good jokes in about equal measure. A gnomish bard will rarely be short of an audience, or a bed.
- **The House of Inspired Hands**, Gond's temple, is popular with gnomish inventors. **Garl Glittergold** keeps a smaller shrine.

## Tools and languages
- **Calligrapher's Supplies:** the scribes and copyists of the city's temples, wizards and guilds always need a fine hand.
- **Shawm, bagpipes and zulkoon:** loud, unusual and memorable. A zulkoon in particular is a rare sight on the Sword Coast. ⚠ verify the zulkoon's origin if it matters.
- **Gnomish, Common Sign Language, Common.**
