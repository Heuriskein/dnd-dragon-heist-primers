# Hadrian Liadon
{: .no_toc }
*Elf fighter, gambler, and charming liar*

You've talked your way into card games, caravans and jobs you had no business holding. Waterdeep is the biggest table on the Sword Coast, and you know how to read the players.

## You know how to work a room
*Because: Charlatan background · Persuasion, Deception and Insight*

You know the [Nobility](../lore/nobility.html) page at **Proficient** depth. The highlights:

- Waterdeep's nobles have money and pride but no legal power over commoners. Flattery opens more doors than threats.
- Address a noble as "Lord" or "Lady" plus the house name until invited to do otherwise. Never call in a debt in public.
- Approach the **steward**, not the lord. And make friends with the servants, who know everything.
- New money overspends to prove itself, and old money sneers at it. Both are easy marks for someone who tells them what they want to hear.
- Notable houses: **Cassalanter** (bankers, famously generous), **Wands** (wizards), **Amcathra** (horses), **Rosznar** (wine, rebuilding a ruined name), **Neverember** (the ousted Open Lord's family; his son Renaer is well liked).

## You know where the games are
*Because: gambler by temperament · Sleight of Hand · Perception*

From [Places](../lore/places.html) and [Underworld](../lore/underworld.html):

- **Gambling is legal and everywhere.** There's dice in the Dock Ward taverns, wagering on the games at the **Field of Triumph**, and private card salons in the **Sea Ward** with high stakes and noble guests.
- **Cheating is not legal**, but the people you cheat rarely go to the magistrates. They go to people like the **Xanathar Guild**, whose thugs run protection and debt collection across the Dock Ward.
- **The Yawning Portal** is where adventurers drink and boast. It's quieter since the [Death Curse](../lore/death-curse.html), and the remaining crowd is more careful, and more willing to bet on long odds.
- **Fences** pay a third of value. Useful when you've won something you'd rather turn into coin.

## You know caravan work and who competes for it
*Because: years as a (self-appointed) caravan guard · Intimidation*

From [Guilds & Trade](../lore/guilds-trade.html) and [Factions](../lore/factions.html):

- Caravans muster in the **Southern Ward** and hire guards each season. A writ from a known guard company gets you hired. In Waterdeep, nobody checks too carefully whose name is on it.
- Unlike Cormyr, Waterdeep doesn't require adventurers to carry a **charter**. Your Cormyrean guild papers impress a few people and mean nothing legally.
- **The Zhentarim** undercut honest guard companies on caravan work, and in Waterdeep they're trying hard to look respectable. You've seen the "good manners" up close, and you've seen what's underneath them.
- **The Harpers** keep to the shadows, but you've done right by them, and they know it. Harpers recognize each other by a small silver harp pin that's rarely worn openly. You're not one of them, but a Harper in Waterdeep might treat you as a friend.

## You had an education whether you wanted one or not
*Because: Arcana proficiency · a scholarly Evereskan upbringing*

You know the [Arcana](../lore/arcana.html) and [Death Curse](../lore/death-curse.html) pages at **Proficient** depth:

- **The Watchful Order of Magists and Protectors** licenses and polices spellcasters. Charming someone with magic inside the city walls is a crime, which is one more reason you rely on the ordinary kind of charm.
- **Blackstaff Tower** is home to the city's archmage, **Vajra Safahr**. The Blackstaff has always been the Lords' magical right hand.
- **The Death Curse** touches only those who have been raised from the dead. Ordinary death still works; it's just final now. No spell has broken the wasting.
- With death permanent, adventurers have become cautious. That means fewer rivals for good jobs.

## You know your own people
*Because: Elf of Evereska · follower of Erevan Ilesere*

From [Peoples of Waterdeep](../lore/races-cultures.html) and [Religion](../lore/religion.html):

- Waterdeep's elves are few: artists, scholars, diplomats. Many have **Evereskan** connections. Word travels between elven households, and a well-born runaway is exactly the sort of gossip that travels.
- The **Seldarine** have small shrines in the city rather than great temples. Followers of **Erevan Ilesere** keep no temple at all. Erevan is honored with pranks, gambles and stories, and his faithful tend to recognize each other.
- Waterdeep tolerates almost any faith that obeys the law. Nobody here will mind your god, though a few may watch their purses.

## Languages
- **Elvish:** opens doors among Waterdeep's elves, and lets you talk freely in front of most humans.
- **Draconic:** the old language of magic and dragons. Wizards' texts and some old inscriptions use it.
- **Common.**

## Your story
- You're a long way from Evereska. Your family would want you back. Elves are patient, and Waterdeep is a city where Evereskan visitors are not unusual.
- You've annoyed gamblers and officials in other towns. Waterdeep is big enough to hide in, and busy enough that old faces turn up.
