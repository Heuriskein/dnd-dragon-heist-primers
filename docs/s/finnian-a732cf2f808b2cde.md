## Where the curse comes from
*Because: Investigation proficiency. You know people who know things.*

The temples and wizards are still arguing about causes. The docks already know where the money is going: **Chult**, the jungle peninsula far to the south, beyond Amn and Calimshan.

- Ships quietly chartered with city coin have sailed for **Port Nyanzaru**, Chult's great harbor. Some of the crews haven't come back.
- Smugglers and fences have noticed buyers paying silly money for anything out of Chult: charms, idols, maps, "cures."
- The few people in the know keep their mouths shut, because a secret that size is worth something.

You don't know *what* in Chult is behind the curse. Only that every serious trail points there.
