# Waterdeep: What You Know

Welcome to Waterdeep, the City of Splendors. These pages hold what your character knows about the city when the campaign begins.

## How to read your primer
1. Start with **[What Every Resident Knows](primers/00-every-resident.html)**. All characters know this.
2. Then read **your own primer** below. It holds what you know because of your skills, background and ancestry.
3. The **Lore** pages are a reference library. Your primer tells you which parts your character knows well. You can read the rest, but your character may not know it.

Everything here is what people *believe*. Rumors can be wrong, and the city has secrets.
