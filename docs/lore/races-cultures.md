# Peoples of Waterdeep

Waterdeep is overwhelmingly human, but every people of Faerûn lives here, and few people care about ancestry as long as you pay your rent.

## Halflings
- Halflings live all over the city. Many have been here for generations. They run inns, bakeries, and shops, and keep many of the city's kitchens and cellars working.
- They rarely cluster in a separate quarter, but they look after each other, and a halfling can usually find a meal and a warm floor in another halfling's home.
- Halflings are underestimated, which some of them use to their advantage. The Shard Shunners, a gang of halfling thieves in the Dock Ward, are an example. ⚠ verify.
- A small shrine to **Yondalla** serves the faithful, but most halflings worship at home and through hospitality.

## Gnomes
- Gnomes are drawn to the city's workshops, alchemists and wizards. Many work in the Trades Ward as tinkers, jewelers and lens-makers.
- Gnomes respect clever work and good jokes in about equal measure. A halfling who speaks Gnomish will find gnomes surprisingly warm.
- The House of Inspired Hands, Gond's temple, is popular among gnomish inventors.

## Dwarves
- Dwarves come for trade, as smiths and stonemasons, and as guards. Some clans keep houses in the city.
- Many work for the guilds or as mercenaries; dwarven ale houses in the Southern and Trades Wards are proud places.

## Elves and half-elves
- Elves are a small population, often artists, scholars or diplomats. Half-elves are common and fit easily into city life.
- Many of Waterdeep's elves have ties to **Evereska**, the hidden elven city in the mountains to the east. It's beautiful, ancient, heavily warded, and famously conservative. Evereskan families expect their children to spend their early centuries in study and duty.
- Elves in the city worship the **Seldarine**, the elven pantheon. Corellon Larethian leads it. Sehanine guides the dead. **Erevan Ilesere**, the trickster, is beloved by elves who can't sit still and distrusted by those who prefer they did.
- Elves live long enough that a family can lose track of a wayward son for decades. They also live long enough to come looking eventually.

## Others
- Dragonborn, tieflings, half-orcs, tabaxi and stranger folk all live in the city. Some get suspicious looks, but city law protects them like anyone else.
- **Kenku** are common in the Dock Ward as messengers and petty criminals, and are widely distrusted.
