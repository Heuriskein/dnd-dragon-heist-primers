# Lore
A reference library about Waterdeep. Each page is split into tiers:
- **Common knowledge:** anyone in the city knows this.
- **Proficient:** someone with the relevant skill or background knows this.
- **Expert:** someone with expertise or a deep personal tie knows this.

Your primer tells you which tiers your character knows.
