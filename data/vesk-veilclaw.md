## Backstory / traits / faction
- **Backstory (summary):** Clan Veilclaw, black-scaled dragonborn of fog-choked marshes, called cruel by blood. At nine he was caught stealing bread; an old man traveling with a caravan, trailed by **seven golden canaries**, paid for it and told him "Scales are what you are born with. Honor is what you choose," then vanished. He trained at the **House of the Drowned Lantern** (half-sunken monastery on an old elven shrine; founder **Abbess Ilyrith Dawnmoss**, ancient elf; "shadow is where you stand to protect the light"). Picked locks for puzzles' sake; **Brother Korvash** (one-horned bronze dragonborn, ex-soldier) taught him the spear; learned Elvish copying lines as punishment. Sent off at 22 with a **platinum dragon pendant**: "Go be a useful one." Carries a notebook of unanswered questions.
- **Personality:** curious, collects stories; drawn to mysteries, locks, sealed letters; fascinated by shadow, calm in the dark; dry teasing humor.
- **Ideal:** Truth and Freedom: nobody decides what you are before you've shown them.
- **Bond:** the old man with the seven canaries. Vesk is certain he was Bahamut, isn't quite sure, can't let it go.
- **Flaw:** can't let go of mysteries, grudges, or the need to be seen as more than his scales.
