## Backstory (from sheet)
- **Personality:** Extremely charming and a little sneaky, Hadrian isn't super bright but uses his charm to get through situations.
- **Ideals:** Loves adventure and new stories, believes the only fights you win are the ones you avoid.
- **Bonds:** Cares deeply about his family but hated his boring life; a follower of Erevan Ilesere.
- **Flaws:** Huge gambler, loves taking risks.
- Second son of a strict high-elf family in Evereska, who expected a century of arcane scholarship from him. He ran off with a pouch of gold and became a wandering tavern gambler.
- He won a bodyguard writ, a greatsword and chainmail from a deserting caravan guard, forged his own name onto the writ, and talked his way into the post.
- On a Sword Coast caravan run, he cheated at dice with a traveler. Afterward he found his purse emptied except for a silver starburst token, Erevan's symbol. Nobody in the caravan had ever heard of the traveler.
- **Allies:** Not a Harper, but on good terms with them.
- **Organizations:** Adventurers' guild in Cormyr; writs of employment with a few guard companies.
- **Enemies:** Dislikes the Zhentarim. Has run afoul of other gamblers and town officials. His parents would haul him home if they found him.
