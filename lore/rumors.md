# Rumors

The rumor pool. This module is not tiered. Each rumor is a `## <id>` section. The build draws a random set for each character. They hear 1, plus 1 with Investigation proficiency, plus 1 more with expertise. Adding or removing a rumor redraws everyone.

Lines starting with `DM:` are notes for the DM. The build strips them, so they never reach a page.

## laeral-carriage
A lamplighter on the Castle Ward rounds swears he's seen the Open Lord's plain black carriage cross to **Blackstaff Tower** after midnight, three nights running, and come back before dawn.
DM: approved. Decide whether it's true, and what she'd be doing there. One of three competing Laeral rumors.

## laeral-errand
A Watchful Order apprentice swears the Open Lord rode out the North Gate before dawn a couple of months back, cloaked and with only two companions, bound for **Silverymoon** to ask her sisters' help against the Death Curse. The Lords are keeping it quiet so the city doesn't panic.
DM: approved. ALLOW-LAERAL-CURSE: the DM chose to let this rumor tie her absence to the curse. It's a red herring (she's fighting it, not suffering it). Points north-east on purpose, away from Chult.

## laeral-quarrel
The Open Lord didn't step back from public life; she was taken. After a shouting match in council, one of the Masked Lords lured her into an **anti-magic field**, and she's been held ever since somewhere beneath Castle Waterdeep while the Lords argue over what to do with her.
DM: approved. One of three competing Laeral rumors.

## temple-draught
The **Temple of Beauty** is said to have a draught that slows the wasting. A vial costs a harbor moon, and the priests of Sune only sell it to families with a name.
DM: approved. A priest's private side business, and largely a red herring. One priest of Sune sells it under the temple's name without the temple's blessing.
DM: The draught is a strong restorative with a mild euphoric. The wasting *feels* slower for a few days but isn't. Buyers swear by it because the first doses help.
DM: Tie-in 1, supply: the rare key ingredient is smuggled in by the Zhentarim, selling to desperate nobles under their respectable face. Shows how Manshoon's people make money and friends. Gives xanathar-daylight a landing: the Xanathar Guild has noticed the Zhents moving into a lucrative trade.
DM: Tie-in 2, client ledger: the priest keeps a ledger of buyers. House Gralhund is on it (a once-raised servant or relative who is wasting). Investigating gives the party a legitimate reason to visit Gralhund Villa well before Chapters 3-4, so they know the household and layout when it matters.
DM: Best as a Chapter 2 thread. If the party runs Trollskull Manor, the priest may offer to sell through their tavern, or a buyer's servant talks over drinks.
DM: Not used: the Open Lord's household buying vials (it would move the Laeral reveal earlier).
