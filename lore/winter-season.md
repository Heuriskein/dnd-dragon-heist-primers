# Winter in Waterdeep

## Common
- Winters are hard: snow, ice and freezing winds off the Sea of Swords. Shipping slows, and parts of the harbor freeze.
- The city turns inward. Taverns are full, nobles hold their winter balls, and the poor of the Field Ward and Dock Ward struggle with cold and the price of firewood.
- **Midwinter** is the great festival at the turn of the year: feasting, gift-giving and noble masquerades.
- This year the [Death Curse](death-curse.html) hangs over everything. Midwinter will be quieter, and many families are mourning someone who wasted away.

## Proficient
- Dock work dries up in winter. Idle sailors and laborers fill the taverns, and petty crime rises.
- Firewood and coal merchants get rich; temples hand out food and blankets.
- The Watch works shorter patrols in hard weather, and certain kinds of business take advantage.
