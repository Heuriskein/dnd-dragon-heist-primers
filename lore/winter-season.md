# Winter in Waterdeep

## Common
- Winters are hard: snow, ice and freezing winds off the Sea of Swords. Shipping slows, and parts of the harbor freeze.
- The city turns inward. Taverns are full, nobles hold their winter balls, and the poor of the Field Ward and Dock Ward struggle with cold and the price of firewood.
- **Midwinter** is the great festival at the turn of the year: feasting, gift-giving and noble masquerades.
- This year the [Death Curse](death-curse.html) hangs over everything. Midwinter will be quieter, and many families are mourning someone who wasted away.
- **Rumors going around this winter** (not all of them true):
  - Laeral Silverhand has left the city on some secret errand. (Or: she's quarrelling with the Masked Lords. Or: she's shut away in Blackstaff Tower on some great working of magic.)
  - A temple in the Sea Ward has a cure, but it only sells to nobles.
  - The city's coffers are nearly empty, and the Lords will raise taxes after Midwinter.
  - The Zhentarim have been buying friends: dinners with guildmasters, donations to temples, offers of cheap guard work.
  - The Xanathar Guild is jumpier than usual. Its thugs have been seen in daylight in places they don't normally go.
  - Someone is supposedly close to finding Neverember's hidden fortune.
  - A noble house is supposedly in trouble with its creditors. Different taverns name different houses.
  - The harbor ice has trapped a ship from Baldur's Gate whose crew won't say what they're carrying.

## Proficient
- Dock work dries up in winter. Idle sailors and laborers fill the taverns, and petty crime rises.
- Firewood and coal merchants get rich; temples hand out food and blankets.
- The Watch works shorter patrols in hard weather, and certain kinds of business take advantage.
