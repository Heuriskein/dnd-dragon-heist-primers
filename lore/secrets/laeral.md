# A theory about the Open Lord

Nobody has said it out loud, and you can't prove it. But you've been turning the pieces over, and they fit uncomfortably well:

- The Death Curse wastes away **anyone who has ever been raised from the dead**, and nobody else.
- **Laeral Silverhand** is centuries old. The old histories of Mystra's Chosen speak of her having **returned**.
- The Open Lord, who used to be everywhere, began to **vanish from public life about a month after the curse struck**. Most people never connected the two. But the wasting is slow, and slowest in the strong. If it touched someone of her power, a month is about how long you'd expect it to take to start showing.

Then again:

- The histories use the word "returned" several times, but whether that implies death is a leap.
- A month is a long gap. Plenty of things could take the Open Lord out of public view, and the city is full of other explanations. You've heard the rumors too.

Your best guess is that **Laeral is wasting**. It would explain the silence, and the fortune the city has spent hunting a cure. But it's a guess built on old stories and timing, not on anything you've seen.

If you're right, saying so publicly could shake the city, and whoever is keeping her condition quiet won't thank you. If you're wrong, you've slandered the Open Lord.
