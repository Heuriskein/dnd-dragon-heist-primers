# Elves of Waterdeep

## Common
- Elves are a small population in the city, often artists, scholars or diplomats. Half-elves are common and fit easily into city life.

## Proficient
- Many of Waterdeep's elves have ties to **Evereska**, the hidden elven city in the hills far to the east. It's beautiful, ancient, heavily warded, and famously conservative. Evereskan families expect their children to spend their early centuries in study and duty.
- Elves in the city worship the **Seldarine**, the elven pantheon. Corellon Larethian leads it. Sehanine guides the dead. **Erevan Ilesere**, the trickster, is beloved by elves who can't sit still and distrusted by those who prefer they did.

## Expert
- Word travels between elven households, and a well-born runaway is exactly the sort of gossip it carries.
- The Seldarine have small shrines in the city rather than great temples. Followers of Erevan keep no temple at all. He is honored with pranks, gambles and stories, and his faithful tend to recognize each other.
- Elves live long enough that a family can lose track of a wayward child for decades. They also live long enough to come looking eventually.
