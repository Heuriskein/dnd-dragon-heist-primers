# Factions

The great organizations of the Sword Coast all keep a presence in Waterdeep. This page covers what is publicly known. Members of a faction will learn more from their contacts in play.

## Common
- **The Harpers** are a secretive network who oppose tyranny and protect the weak. They're more legend than fact to most folk, and they like it that way.
- **The Lords' Alliance** is a coalition of Sword Coast cities, including Waterdeep, Neverwinter and Baldur's Gate, which work together for mutual defense and trade.
- **The Order of the Gauntlet** is a holy order of knights and warriors who hunt evil. They're earnest, brave, and sometimes too eager.
- **The Emerald Enclave** are druids, rangers and others who protect the natural world. They're rarely seen in the city.
- **The Zhentarim** are mercenaries and traders with a dark history, now working hard to look respectable in Waterdeep.

## Proficient

- Faction membership is mostly informal: favors, contacts and shared goals. Members are expected to help each other.
- Harpers communicate through coded messages, and pass recognition through a small silver harp pin, which is rarely worn openly.
- The Lords' Alliance keeps an embassy and agents in Waterdeep. Waterdeep is its most powerful member, and the others resent that.
- The Zhentarim in Waterdeep offer guard work and caravan protection at reasonable prices. Their critics say it's a protection racket with good manners.

## Expert

- The factions don't trust each other, and some are outright enemies. The Harpers and the Zhentarim have fought each other for generations, and few Harpers believe the Black Network's new respectable face. Individual members sometimes cooperate when their interests line up, but nobody forgets which side the other is on.
- Each faction pays in different coin: information, favors, rank, a place to sleep. They all expect results.
- Faction-specific details will come from your faction contact in play. Ask your DM.
